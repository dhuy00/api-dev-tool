# Workspace and Team Management

## Overview

The workspace module provides governance for personal and team assets.

## Core Responsibilities

- Personal workspace creation.
- Team workspace creation and management.
- Workspace invitation and role assignment.
- Workspace ownership transfer.

## Roles

- Owner
- Admin
- Editor
- Viewer

## Permission Handling

Role separation and action scope are documented in the permission matrix.

## MVP Positioning

The MVP should support personal workspaces and a minimum of team workspace administration with clear role definitions.
